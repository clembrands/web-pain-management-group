## Not used, and why

- **Program values** on the scorecard, audit, pain-improvement, and financial slides
  (17–29). They are real results for four partner programs, including net operating income
  and prescribing rates. Only the names of the measures are published.
- **Slides 33–40**, the closing template ("illustrative monthly data"): the +74% referral
  growth, the sample scorecard, and the "7–14 day access goal" are placeholders, not facts.
- **"50+ partner locations"** (slide 13): conflicts with the homework's 40 partnerships and
  about 68 care locations. Asked on PMG's confirmation list.
- **53% from the U.S. Pain Foundation** (slide 5): the report could not be found at a
  working link. It can be added once PMG or we locate the source.
- **The deck's images** (map, photograph, icons): Canva does not allow export from the
  shared link, the previews are too small for the web, and the photograph is stock of
  unknown license. The diagrams were rebuilt in the site's design instead, which also keeps
  their text readable to search and AI engines.
- **Direct phone numbers and emails** for the leaders (slide 34): not published without
  PMG's say-so.
