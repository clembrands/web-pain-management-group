# Photography direction for client review

The 1H navy, blue, Poppins typography, and rounded imagery remain the visual foundation.
The review interiors now use photo heroes, captioned image cards, and supporting care photography.
Legal pages and the review inventory intentionally remain typographic.

| Area | Review treatment | Final photography needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hospital partnerships and resources | Physician/administrator concept image | PMG and hospital leadership working together in a real program |
| Provider, career, and education pages | Consultation concept image | Consented care-team interaction, procedure environment, team collaboration |
| Partner stories | Existing partnership concept photography | Specific partner hospital, care team, and approved interview imagery |
| Clinic pages | Architectural illustration in a photo-sized frame | Actual clinic entrance, exterior signage, interior arrival area |
| Leadership | Portrait illustration in a photo-sized frame | Consistent individual headshots and a leadership group image |
| Contact | Captioned team concept photo alongside inquiry form | Welcoming PMG team image |

Use warm natural light, believable hospital environments, and human interaction.
Keep important faces centered enough for mobile crops; avoid text baked into photographs.
Capture landscape images for heroes/cards and portrait images for biographies.

Existing concept images are visibly labeled. The clinic illustrations are not maps or building
representations, and portrait illustrations do not depict a real team member. The repeated concept
photos establish layout and tone; the final photography should vary by the specific page subject.

The review media mapping lives in src/content/review/media.ts. This is local demo content;
CMS-managed images and final rights/consent checks remain part of the publishing phase.
